# Kiitos

*Kiitos* is Finnish for "thank you". It's a testimonial engine for coaches, creatives and small businesses that lives in your own Netlify account. It collects testimonials through friendly forms, lets you curate them in a private admin, and shows them off as Wall of Love pages, website widgets and Instagram share cards.

## Set up your own copy

[![Deploy to Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/img/deploy/button.svg)](https://app.netlify.com/start/deploy?repository=https://github.com/litolabs/kiitos)

1. **Click the button.** Sign in to Netlify and GitHub (both free) and Netlify copies Kiitos into your own accounts.
2. **Choose your admin password** when Netlify asks for `ADMIN_PASSWORD`.
3. **Open your admin.** When the deploy finishes, go to `your-site.netlify.app/admin` and log in.

Your setup lessons walk you through everything after that, and the **Guide** inside your admin explains every screen.

## Getting updates

When a newer Kiitos is out, a note appears at the top of your Inbox. To update, open Netlify, go to **Deploys**, tap **Trigger deploy**, then **Deploy project**. Kiitos fetches the newest version while it deploys, and your testimonials, forms, walls and settings stay exactly as they are. What changed in each version is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

You don't need to touch your own GitHub copy. Leave it as it is and updates keep arriving through Netlify.
