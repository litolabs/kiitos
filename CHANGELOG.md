# What's new in Kiitos

Every update is included with Kiitos. When a newer version is out, a note appears at the top of your Inbox. To update, open Netlify, go to **Deploys**, tap **Trigger deploy**, then **Deploy project**. Your testimonials, forms, walls and settings stay exactly as they are.

## 0.2.0

The first public release.

- **Collect.** Friendly testimonial forms with your questions, an optional star rating, a cropped photo and clear consent for name and photo.
- **Import.** Bring testimonials in from a screenshot (the AI reads it for you), by hand, from a spreadsheet, or straight from a Senja export.
- **Inbox.** Approve, hide, edit, tag and feature, with undo.
- **Walls of Love.** Public pages grouped by tag, with pins, leave-offs and your own order.
- **Widgets.** Scrolling marquee, avatar strip, rating badge, grid and single card, for any website builder.
- **Share cards.** Branded square, tall and Story images for Instagram.
- **AI helpers.** Suggested highlights, themes and Voice of Customer reports, paid from your Netlify credits.
- **Style presets.** Five one-tap looks with a live preview.
- **Updates.** A note in your Inbox when a newer Kiitos is out, and one redeploy to get it.
